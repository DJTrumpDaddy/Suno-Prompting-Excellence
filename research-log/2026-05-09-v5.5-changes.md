## Confirmed Findings

### Release Date
Suno v5.5 released **March 26, 2026**.

---

### Three Major New Features

#### 1. Voices (Pro/Premier subscribers only)
- Voice cloning: upload or record your own singing voice (15 seconds – 4 minutes)
- Anti-deepfake verification: must speak a random phrase live to verify identity
- Voice is private and locked to your account — no one else can use it
- Once set up, Voice appears in the Create menu
- Prompting with Voice: can add "in my own voice" to style descriptions, or adjust BPM/gender hints
- Was described as an evolution of "Personas" (which captures song style essence)
- Audio Influence sweet spot with Voice: ~40% (preserves vocal character without sacrificing quality)

#### 2. Custom Models (Pro/Premier, up to 3 variants)
- Upload tracks from your original catalog to fine-tune v5.5 to your style
- Creates a personalized version of the model that "sounds more like you"
- Addresses style drift in general models — makes genre reproduction more accurate
- Prompting: standard v5 prompts work the same; Custom Model adds baseline genre/style context

#### 3. My Taste (all users)
- Passive preference learning: Suno tracks your usage patterns (genres, moods, styles)
- Gets more accurate over time — no user action required
- Acts as a soft baseline for generation, not overriding prompts

### Prompting Compatibility — NO BREAKING CHANGES

**All confirmed unchanged from v5:**
- Style block character limit: **1,000 characters** (same)
- Lyrics field character limit: **5,000 characters** (same)
- All metatags work identically
- All style tags work identically
- All v5 prompts are directly compatible

**Quoted confirmation:** "Your prompts, metatags, and style tags all work exactly like v5." — multiple sources

---

### Audio Quality Improvements (v5.5 vs v5)

- Vocal breathing and emotional transitions closer to real human singing
- Mixing soundstage and instrument separation reach professional studio quality
- Significantly improved prompt accuracy — genre prompts follow genre conventions more faithfully
  - Example: "jazz" prompt sounds more authentically jazz, not "pop with jazz chords"
- Chinese/dialect singing recognition and articulation comprehensively enhanced
- Better support for niche styles: Chinese traditional, indie, regional genres

---

### Song Length — UNCHANGED

- Maximum per generation: **8 minutes** (same as v5 on paid plans)
- Free plan: shorter limit (unchanged)
- 12+ minutes achievable through extension chaining (Premier plan)
- v5.5 did NOT increase the per-generation cap beyond 8 minutes

---

### Audio Influence (Inspo) Slider — BEHAVIOR CHANGED SIGNIFICANTLY

This is the most impactful change for prompt engineers.

**Before v5.5 (v5 behavior):**
- Audio Influence was a "vibe extractor" — pulled mood, groove, and texture while changing chord progressions and melodies
- Low-to-mid settings (20–40%) produced a different song that evoked the source's vibe

**In v5.5 (new behavior):**
- Behaves more like Remix — melody gets picked up much more strongly at the same slider values
- Sources describe it as "moved closer to Remix in a less favorable way"

**New recommended zones for v5.5:**

| Range | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 0–5% | Almost kills melodic influence; vibe only |
| 8% | Practical sweet spot — vibe extraction without melody bleed |
| 15–25% | Faint trace of motif |
| 20–30% | Picks up original melody fairly firmly |
| 50%+ | Remix/Mashup territory |
| 70%+ | Near cover feel |
| 40% | Recommended for voice cloning (preserves vocal character) |

**Previously documented zones (v5, now outdated for v5.5):**
- 20–40% = loose texture (now too high; melody bleeds through)
- 41–60% = moderate (now near full Remix)
- 61–75% = strong (now near cover)
- 76–100% = very tight (unchanged — same extreme behavior)

- Definitions, ranges, and behavior unchanged from v5
- Starting point: Weirdness ~50, Style Influence ~60 (unchanged)
- All genre-specific slider recommendations remain valid

### Negative Prompting — Emphasized More in v5.5

- "no X" constraints (e.g., "no autotune", "no synths", "no drums") work well in v5.5
- Community best practice: use 1–2 negative constraints to tighten results
- V5.5's improved prompt accuracy makes negative prompts more effective than in v5

---

### Style Block Best Practices — Community Update

- Previous KB guidance: 8–15 descriptors
- Updated community consensus for v5.5: **4–7 descriptors is the sweet spot**
- Reasoning: v5.5's improved prompt accuracy means fewer, more precise descriptors outperform long lists
- Character limit unchanged at 1,000
