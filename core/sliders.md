# Suno v5 Slider Parameters

> **Status:** Research in progress — populated 2026-04-22
> **Scope:** Suno v5 only. Slider names and behavior may differ from v4.

---

## Overview

Suno v5 exposes slider controls that modify generation behavior. These interact with — but do not override — the style block and lyrics.

## Known Sliders

*(Exact slider names and ranges to be confirmed from research — current entries are based on reported behavior)*

### Creativity
- **What it does:** Controls how closely Suno follows the style block vs. exploring variations
- **Low (1-3):** Very faithful to the style descriptors; less surprising, more predictable
- **Mid (4-6):** Balanced — recommended starting point for most genres
- **High (7-10):** More experimental; may blend genres or introduce unexpected elements
- **Genre guidance:**
  - Classical, acoustic: 3-5 (keep it structured)
  - Lo-fi, ambient: 6-8 (some drift is desirable)
  - Experimental, avant-garde: 8-10

### Clarity
- **What it does:** Controls the sharpness/definition of the mix
- **Low (1-3):** Warmer, muddier, more lo-fi character
- **Mid (4-6):** Balanced production
- **High (7-10):** Crisp, modern, well-defined mix
- **Genre guidance:**
  - Lo-fi hip-hop: 2-4
  - Pop, EDM: 7-9
  - Metal: 6-8 (clarity without harshness)
  - Acoustic folk: 5-7

## Common Mistakes

- **Max creativity for all genres** — loses the genre identity; sounds chaotic
- **Max clarity for lo-fi** — destroys the defining sonic texture
- **Min creativity for experimental genres** — produces generic output
- **Using sliders to fix structural problems** — sliders affect tone/style, not section structure; use metatags for structure issues
